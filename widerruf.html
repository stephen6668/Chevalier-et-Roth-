14-day right of withdrawal

Consumers may generally withdraw from a distance purchase within 14 calendar days without giving a reason. For goods, the period normally begins when the consumer, or a person designated by the consumer, receives the goods.

How to withdraw

Send a clear statement of withdrawal to: [ENTER CUSTOMER-SERVICE EMAIL] or the business address listed in the Legal Notice. The notice must be sent before the withdrawal period expires.

Returning the goods

After notifying us of the withdrawal, return the goods without undue delay and no later than 14 days. Return address: [ENTER RETURN ADDRESS]. The direct cost of returning the goods is borne by the customer unless Chevalier & Roth agrees otherwise or the law requires otherwise.

Condition of returned goods

Customers may inspect and try on clothing as they would in a shop. The customer may be responsible for diminished value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of the goods.

Refunds

Eligible payments, including standard delivery costs where required by law, are refunded without undue delay and generally no later than 14 days after receipt of the withdrawal notice. For goods, reimbursement may be withheld until the goods are returned or proof of return is provided. Refunds are normally made using the same payment method used for the original transaction.
