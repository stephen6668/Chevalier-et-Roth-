Payment Information

Secure payments

The shop is prepared for Stripe Checkout. When activated, customers use a Stripe-hosted secure payment form embedded inside the checkout page. Chevalier & Roth does not directly store full payment card details.

Accepted methods

The exact payment methods available to a customer are displayed by Stripe at checkout and may depend on device, country, currency and Stripe account configuration.

Currency and VAT

Payments are charged in euro (EUR). Product prices shown on the website include applicable VAT. The current storefront configuration uses a 17% Luxembourg standard VAT display.

Payment confirmation

An order should only be treated as paid after successful payment confirmation from Stripe. A failed or cancelled payment does not create a completed paid order.
