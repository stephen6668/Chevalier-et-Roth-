Cookie & Storage Policy

Essential storage only by default

The current storefront uses browser storage for functions such as the shopping bag and interface state, and Appwrite may use session-related storage needed for authentication. These functions are intended to be strictly necessary for services requested by the user.

Analytics and marketing

No optional analytics or advertising tracker should be activated unless it is first documented here and, where required, valid consent is obtained before the technology is set.

Payments

When Stripe Checkout is activated and a customer proceeds to payment, Stripe's own technologies and privacy information apply on the Stripe-hosted checkout page.
