OUR STORY

Designed in Luxembourg.
Produced in Italy.

CR

A modern European maison.

Chevalier & Roth was founded in Luxembourg in 2026 with a clear idea: create elegant clothing that feels refined without sacrificing comfort. The brand focuses on balanced proportions, restrained colours and pieces that can remain part of a wardrobe beyond a single season.

Design and brand direction are developed in Luxembourg, while production is based in Italy. The first collection brings together men’s and women’s pieces including half-zips, tailored trousers and refined knitwear, positioned as premium European fashion with a timeless identity.
